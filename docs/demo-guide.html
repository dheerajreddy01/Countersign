What to say over slides 2 and 3

0:00–0:15 · on slide 2
"A chargeback costs a merchant about $128. They win 54% of the disputes they fight, but fight only 8%, because building a defense by hand isn't worth it on any single case. And now AI agents buy on people's behalf, which breaks 'I didn't authorize this.' Countersign answers both."
0:15–1:00 · on slide 3, pointing left to right
"A dispute starts as a webhook. Our gateway, written in Go, verifies PayPal's signature, drops duplicates, and publishes it to Redpanda, a Kafka-compatible event bus. Everything after that is an event. A normalizer turns PayPal's payload into a dispute; the case service writes it to Postgres. The evidence builder assembles the bundle: the order, delivery, support history, and the agent's mandate from our signed, hash-chained ledger. Then the AI service, in Python, scores the odds of winning, weighs the expected value of fighting against refunding, and has an LLM draft the rebuttal behind a grounding guard: every sentence must cite evidence. The merchant approves in the console; the executor carries the action out against PayPal's Disputes API, and the outcome flows back onto the case. Each step is idempotent and retried, and a record that can't be processed goes to a dead-letter topic instead of vanishing. It all runs on Render: six services and a managed Postgres."

Checklist

Before you hit record

1 · Wake the free services

Each sleeps after 15 idle minutes and takes about a minute to wake. All three should print 200.

for s in console gateway mandate; do
  curl -s -o /dev/null -w "$s %{http_code}\n" \
    https://countersign-$s-yhhv.onrender.com/healthz
done

2 · Open three tabs

  • Console board, logged in as merchant
  • Sandbox Resolution Center as the buyer, on PP-R-TMY-10198515
  • This guide, on slide 2 for the opening

3 · Leave the case service alone

  • No redeploys and no env changes on countersign-caseservice before recording.
  • If cases re-score on their own or drafts flip, use Restart service and wait for one instance.

4 · Protect your cases

  • Approve on Lucas Moreau · Standing Desk Frame only.
  • Leave the real dispute un-actioned for judges.
  • Don't rehearse the approval: it's one-way.
